The histograms `pwg_nloqcd_??.dat` and `pwg_nlops_??.dat` in `direc_simulation/` produced by direct simulation of the polarised signals, are taken from
`./Results/NLOQCD/POWHEG/` and `./Results/NLOQCDPS/POWHEG/qcd/`
in [https://github.com/multibosons/polarisationcomparison](https://github.com/multibosons/polarisationcomparison).

The reweighted ones, saved in `reweighting/`, are simulated for this paper.


- `pwg_lo_ll`     -> LO without shower
- `pwg_lows_ll`   -> LO + Sudakov without shower
- `pwg_nloqcd_ll` -> NLO + Sudakov without shower
- `pwg_nlops_ll`  -> NLOPS
